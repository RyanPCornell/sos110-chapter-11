Weather vs. Climate

Weather is what happens over hours or days; climate is the long-term average over decades. The single most common climate misconception comes from confusing the two. Both graphs below let you zoom in and out — watch how the story flips depending on your window.

📅
Lab 1 · One Real Year of Daily Weather
Real data: daily high temperatures in Minneapolis, Minnesota, 2022 (Open-Meteo / ERA5 reanalysis). Zoom into the genuine cold snap of May 12–21 and the temperature is clearly falling — from 32°C (90°F) to 12°C (54°F) in ten days. But zoom out to the whole year and you see the obvious truth: spring really was warming into summer.
