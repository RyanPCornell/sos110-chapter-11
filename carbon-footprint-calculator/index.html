🔬Methodology — How This Calculator Works▾

This tool estimates the greenhouse gases you cause in a year, expressed as kilograms or tonnes of CO₂-equivalent (CO₂e) — a common unit that rolls methane and nitrous oxide into carbon dioxide using their 100-year global-warming potentials (IPCC AR6). It focuses on the choices the sliders represent (driving, flying, diet, home energy, purchases); it does not include shared/public emissions (roads, government, military) or the emissions embodied in services you don't control.

Avoiding double-counting: Streaming and AI use electricity in two places — on your device (already in the Home Energy kWh slider) and in off-site data centres and networks. The Streaming and AI line items count only the off-site (upstream) share, and use the US-average grid intensity because those data centres don't run on your home utility plan. Drawing system boundaries carefully like this is a core practice of lifecycle assessment.
